Open a new council proposal (`P<n>`, version 1).

Every section is required and MUST be non-empty:
- `title`: short name of the solution.
- `summary`: the solution in a few sentences.
- `solution`: the full design — what changes, where, and how.
- `evidence`: findings that support it (file:line references, command output, measurements, documentation); separate what you verified from what you infer.
- `tradeoffs`: costs, risks, and rejected alternatives.
- `implementation`: concrete steps to carry it out.

Independent analysis: at most one proposal per member. Debate: open a new proposal only when no existing one can be revised into your position.
