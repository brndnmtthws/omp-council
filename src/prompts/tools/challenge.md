Object to an open proposal. The challenge (`C<n>`) pins the proposal's current version.

- `proposal`: proposal id.
- `objection`: the specific flaw and why it matters.
- `evidence`: optional supporting references or output.

Challenging retracts your own acceptance of that proposal. While your challenge stays `open` you cannot accept the proposal; a revision that addresses it, or your own `council_resolve`, clears the way.
