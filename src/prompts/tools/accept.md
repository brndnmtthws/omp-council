Endorse the exact current version of one open proposal.

- `proposal`: proposal id.
- `version`: MUST equal the proposal's current version.
- `rationale`: at least 30 characters — why you would defend this version on your own.

You hold one endorsement at a time; accepting another proposal replaces it. Refused while your own `open` challenge against the proposal stands. Accept only what you would defend independently, never to conform or end the debate.

When every member accepts the same version, the council enters independent verification.
